Your Abraham is not one person

The fan above is drawn to scale: one ring per generation, doubling each time. By the 29th ring (around AD 1116) the tree has more slots than there were people alive on Earth, and by the 37th (around AD 876) more than have ever lived. The tree folds in on itself long before AD 786: cousins marry cousins, lines cross and recross, and the same people fill many slots at once. Geneticists call it pedigree collapse.

The practical consequence: almost everyone alive in 786 who has any living descendants, within the populations your ancestors mixed with, is your ancestor. Your Abraham is a crowd. Abraham himself is remembered as one man only because somebody wrote his name down.

Your Jesus, and what nobody can chart

Someone born in 786 could not have pictured you. Hold that thought while reading the questions below: it is the honest answer to all of them. Where something can actually be said, it is marked.

The little that can actually be said about 3186

Not prophecy: physics with long lead times.

  • The coastlineThe IPCC's sixth assessment puts committed sea-level rise over the next 2,000 years at 2–3 m if warming holds at 1.5 °C, 2–6 m at 2 °C, and 19–22 m at 5 °C. Accra, Lagos, Abidjan and Dakar are all coastal cities. Where your Jesus is born depends partly on decisions made in your own generation.
  • No ice ageOrbital forcing rules out the next glaciation for at least 50,000 years, with or without emissions. Your descendants inherit a warm world either way.
  • A different north starPolaris is closest to the pole around 2100 and then drifts off. By 3186 the pole is wandering toward Errai (Gamma Cephei), which takes over around 4000. Your descendants will not navigate by your star.
  • Halley's cometReturns in 2061 and then roughly every 76 years. About fifteen more visits fall between you and your Matthew Jesus; about twenty-one before your Luke Jesus.
  • The language gapOld English, the tongue of 786, is unreadable to a native English speaker without study. Twi and Hausa of 786 are entirely unrecorded. Expect the same drift forward.

The machinery

Matthew 1 lists Abraham to Jesus in three sets of fourteen, about 40 generations. Luke 3 runs the other way and lists about 56. Both are run at a central 30 years a generation, with a 25-to-35-year range, anchored on a 1986 birth.

The arithmetic

Backward: 1986 − (40 × 30) = 786; 1986 − (56 × 30) = 306. Forward: 1986 + 1200 = 3186; 1986 + 1680 = 3666. The two gospels stay 16 generations, about 480 years, apart in either direction.

The template's own looseness

Tradition puts Abraham around 2000 BC, some 2,000 years before Jesus, yet 40 or 56 generations at 30 years gives 1,200 or 1,680. The Gospel lists were never meant as arithmetic. Neither is this page.
